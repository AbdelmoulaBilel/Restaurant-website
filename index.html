FAQ

Good to know before you come

Do I need a reservation?

Walk-ins are welcome and we always keep bar seats free, but Friday and Saturday evenings are usually full. Booking online takes 30 seconds and gets you free house bread.

Do you have vegetarian and gluten-free options?

Yes. Several pizzas, the halloumi burger and most starters are vegetarian. Any pizza can be made on a gluten-free base for +$3. Our kitchen handles flour, so we can't guarantee zero traces for celiac guests.

Can you host a group or private event?

Our private room seats up to 40 guests with set menus from $35 per person. Choose "11+" in the booking form or call us.

Is it kid-friendly?

Very. We have high chairs, a kids' pizza for $9, and the open kitchen is a show on its own.

Do you do takeaway or delivery?

Takeaway yes: call us and it's ready in about 20 minutes. We don't deliver ourselves, so the pizza arrives the way it should.
